# How to update the Chris' Ice Cream website

You don't need to know any code. Everything is done with simple forms.

## Signing in

1. Go to **https://app.pagescms.org**
2. Click **Sign in with GitHub** and enter your GitHub email and password.
3. Click **chris-ice-cream**.

You'll see four sections on the left: **Closure notice**, **Hours & contact**, **Food menu**, and **Ice cream & smoothies**.

When you're done with any change, click **Save** (top right). The website updates in about a minute.

---

## Closed today? (snow, ice, power outage, holiday)

1. Open **1. Closure notice / announcement**.
2. Turn ON **Show the notice on the website**.
3. Type of notice: **Closed**
4. Write the message in English and in Spanish.
5. Optional: choose a date in **Hide automatically after this date** so it turns itself off.
6. **Save**.

A dark red **CLOSED** banner will appear at the very top of the website.

**When you reopen:** open the notice again, turn **Show** OFF, and **Save**.

For good news (open late, new flavor, etc.) choose type **Info** instead — it shows as a gold banner.

---

## Change a price

1. Open **3. Food menu** (or **4. Ice cream & smoothies**).
2. Click the section (for example **Burritos**), then the item.
3. Change the **Price** — always include the dollar sign, like `$12.49`.
4. **Save**.

## Something ran out

Open the item or flavor and turn on **Sold out**. It stays on the menu with a "Sold out / Agotado" label. Turn it off when it's back.

## Take something off the menu

- **For a while:** turn on **Hide from the menu**. It disappears but nothing is lost — turn it off to bring it back.
- **For good:** use the delete (trash) button on that item.

## Add a new item or flavor

1. Open the section where it belongs.
2. Click **Add** at the bottom of the list.
3. Fill in the name and description in **both English and Spanish**, plus the price.
4. Drag it up or down to put it in the right spot.
5. **Save**.

## Change hours or phone number

Open **2. Hours & contact**, edit, and **Save**. The new hours show everywhere on the site at once.

---

## Tips

- **Made a mistake?** Every save is kept. Ask Chad and he can restore any earlier version.
- **Don't see your change?** Wait one minute, then refresh the page (on a phone, pull down on the page).
- Spanish fields: if one is ever left blank, the Spanish menu will show the English text instead.
